## 007 Remastered 1.6.2: Agent Under Fire on Linux, macOS and Android

- **Agent Under Fire** now runs on **Linux** too, and the launcher offers it on **Linux and macOS** (macOS is untested), with **Android** support in the app. Both games share one renderer now.
- The **Android app** looks like the PC launcher: game tabs, the numbered cards and the Play button. It plays NightFire and Agent Under Fire packages.
- Agent Under Fire: **Noir** keeps only deep reds in colour, the sight tuner's settings are built in, and everything unlocks in LAN multiplayer without touching your save.
- NightFire: a clear message when the renderer cannot start on macOS and Linux.

Downloads: Windows launcher (.exe, both games), Linux launcher (.AppImage), macOS launcher (.dmg) and the Android app (.apk).

No game files are included: the launcher builds the games from your own Xbox disc.
