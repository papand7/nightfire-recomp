## 007: NightFire Remastered 1.4.0

NVIDIA DLSS, a photo mode, screenshots, a performance overlay, an achievements page and more. Download the launcher
below and press Update (or Install).

### Graphics
- **NVIDIA DLSS** on RTX graphics cards: DLAA for the smoothest picture, or Quality, Balanced, Performance and Ultra
  Performance for more frames per second. FSR 2 stays for every other card, and gets Ultra Performance too.

### New
- **Photo mode:** pause and choose Photo Mode (or press F10). Fly the camera anywhere, change the field of view,
  exposure, depth of field and look, and take a clean shot with no HUD.
- **Screenshots:** press F12 to save a picture to the Screenshots folder.
- **Performance overlay:** frames per second, or a full panel with frame times and the resolution the game draws at
  (More Settings > Performance).
- **Achievements page:** all 19 achievements in the game's menus, with your progress (More Settings > Achievements).
- **Quick restart:** Restart in the pause menu restarts the mission at once, and a Restart Mission key can be set.
- **Aim zoom:** choose how far aiming down the sights zooms in.
- **Reset to defaults:** puts all settings back as they were, keeping your keys and saves.
- **Discord** status showing what you're playing (switched on in a later update).

### Launcher
- The Install button now does the whole install; the extra Build button is gone.
