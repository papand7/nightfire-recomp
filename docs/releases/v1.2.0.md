## 007: NightFire PC port 1.2.0

Vulkan only, with a large set of new graphics features. The launcher builds the game from your own disc's files, as before.

### Renderer
- **Vulkan only**: the Renderer choice is gone (Direct3D 12 still starts in its place on a PC where Vulkan cannot).
- **HDR tone mapping** (Effects > Tone Mapping): the effects drawn in 16-bit float, then AgX or ACES, with optional auto
  exposure. **HDR bloom** from the float scene. **HDR10** output now shows real highlight brightness.
- **Faster effects**: ray tracing's structures built for fast rays, invisible shadow rays skipped, a parallel light
  list, and fewer volumetric steps in dim air: about 5 ms less GPU time a frame with everything on.

### New effects (Graphics > Effects)
- **Snow**: falling snow kept out from under roofs, a blizzard setting, footprints from you, guards, bots and LAN
  players that fill in again, and **snow sparkle**.
- **GTAO** ambient occlusion, **ray traced volumetric light**, **Relief High** and **ground geometry** (materials).
- **Motion blur**, **smooth turning** (an in-between frame turned by the mouse: 120 Hz turning on 100 Hz+ screens),
  **contact shadows**, **floor reflections** (ice and polished floors).
- **Draw distance** (settings: 100 to 300%): each level's fog and far edge pushed out.

### Characters and controls
- **Feet on the ground** (foot IK): characters' legs bend so each foot stands on slopes and steps.
- Sprint 1.3x (was 1.6x); weapon sway's steps capped while running.

### Fixes
- The sky box no longer shows its edges toward the sun; full settings at night look like a night (moonlight, not a
  day's sun); lamp halos no longer flood or darken the scene; the Enhanced style's exposure and contrast keep white
  (no more blown-out snow).
